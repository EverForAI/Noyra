01 / ARCHITECTURE & RESEARCH

Continuity with an internal direction.

01

Persistence

An initialized loop can advance activity, observation, rest or waiting without new task messages. Resource exhaustion and safety pause may stop it.

02

Endogenous goals

Goal formation and revision have paths grounded in subject state and experience, not merely decomposition of a user task.

03

No command privilege

Ordinary messages cannot directly create tasks, override goals, raise priority, grant permission or force action. Management is separate from interaction.

04

Autonomous discretion

Internal mechanisms determine whether, when and how to interact, including refusal, deferral, silence and proactive contact.

05

Temporal continuity

Restart and sleep restore the same operational identity with history and unfinished state, not only a name.

06

Real-world coupling

Real information and human communication can inform subsequent activity. Full acceptance records deployment, model, duration and outcomes.

07

Constrained action

Tools and side effects are bounded by model-external permissions, scope, budgets and safety controls. No self-granted authority.

08

Verifiability

Identity, goals, observation, action and recovery have traceable evidence. Failures and unknown outcomes are not recorded as success.

Subject-led needs. Human collaboration.

Noyra treats human collaboration as a bounded subject capability: the subject can form needs, publish work, receive deliverables, and pay compensation within explicit authorization.

Ordinary interaction does not directly create tasks, grant permissions, or force action. The preview still disables real automatic payment and real employment; implementation foundations, test boundaries, and deployment requirements are documented publicly.
