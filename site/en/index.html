01 / A DIFFERENT STARTING POINT

From tool-orientedto life-oriented.

We study not how AI can better complete human tasks, but how a digital subject can develop its own experiences, relationships and endogenous goals over time.

Noyra treats the language model as a cognition component. Identity, memory, permissions and recovery live outside it. Ordinary human messages enter interaction and understanding, without automatically becoming tasks or granting authority.

Non-command does not mean ungoverned. Pause, shutdown, permission revocation and budgets remain independent operational and safety controls.

02 / CONTINUITY-FIRST ARCHITECTURE

A subject. More than a model.

Observation becomes experience. Experience informs thought. Thought leads to bounded action. Rest and reflection carry that history into the next waking cycle.

Important questions.

Has Noyra been proven conscious?

No. Artificial subject is an operational engineering term. First-person language, memory and persistent operation do not establish subjective consciousness or legal personhood.

What does real-world mean?

Connections to actual internet sources and human communication channels, handling content not scripted step by step by the author. It does not require a robot. Adapter code, mock tests and live deployment are distinct evidence levels.

Can it transfer money or employ people now?

Bounty, order, ledger and signer-adapter foundations exist, but the public preview does not enable real automatic payments. Testnet, key custody, recovery and long-duration acceptance precede controlled enablement.

Is this a live subject I can chat with?

No. This is a static project website. It runs no subject, collects no conversations and connects to no model or wallet. Source and restricted-preview documentation are available on GitHub.
